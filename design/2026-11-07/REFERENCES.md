# RescueMesh references

Opened and visually inspected before this direction was generated:

| Awwwards reference | Specific lesson applied |
|---|---|
| [La Gata Perduda interactive map](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/interactive-map-la-gata-perduda) | Place context and one selected story live together. ECSW shows original need beside uncertain landmark context. |
| [Voices from the Frontlines map stories](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/map-story-highlights-voices-of-the-frontlines-1) | A human account remains tied to geographical evidence. The report leads, not an abstract metric. |
| [Listening Together](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/music-data-visualization-listening-together) | One connection can lead within a broader region. Supervisor emphasizes the current need/offer pair rather than a decorative mesh. |
| [Qudrix configurator](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/wizard-qudrix) | Keep the subject stable and scope controls to the current task. Human controls inspect/correct/pause instead of repeating handoff approvals. |

Also consulted [Apple layout](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout) and [typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography) guidance for hierarchy, legibility and progressive disclosure; [T-Mobile brand assets](https://www.t-mobile.com/news/brand-assets) for the owner's magenta color nod; and the official [UTD campus map](https://services.utdallas.edu/download/Parking_Map.pdf) for building terminology. Current generated maps are explicitly schematic, not a reproduction of surveyed campus geometry.

Earlier [Accordion](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/accordion) and [Akaru V4](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/akaru-2) were inspected but the owner rejected the resulting initial directions. They do not authorize reverting to those layouts. A Marshall Fire Map element returned404 and is not claimed as visually studied. No third-party photographs, logos or site code are redistributed in this packet.
